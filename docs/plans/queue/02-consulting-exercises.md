Status: queued; follows a working compact harness.

## Work

Implement one synthetic investigation fixture family, then author Tracks 2–6 around bounded tools, cited evidence, independent checks, engagement controls, and handoff. No live customer access is needed.

## Acceptance

- Stable fixture/event IDs and documented provenance; incomplete and conflicting results.
- Two synthetic engagements for isolation checks.
- Known supported/unsupported claims and independent grading.
- Synthetic injection tests distinguish attempted from executed effects.
- Read-only workflow and optional proposed diff, with no deployment action.
- Second-consultant handoff exercise with a reproducible run record.
- Source citations beside concepts and pinned metadata for adaptations.
